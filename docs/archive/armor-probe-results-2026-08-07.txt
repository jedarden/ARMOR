================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. MULTIPART UPLOAD BROKEN: All 6 multipart upload tests failed with the same
   InternalError wrapping InvalidPart. The parts are being accepted during
   upload_part() (no InvalidPartSize errors), but CompleteMultipartUpload
   cannot find them.

2. NO ALIGNMENT VALIDATION DETECTED: Since all parts - both aligned and
   misaligned - fail with the same InvalidPart error, there is NO evidence
   that ARMOR is enforcing 65536-byte alignment during part upload.

3. PUTOBJECT WORKS FINE: Test 7 (PutObject with misaligned 4837376 B) passed,
   confirming that credentials and basic bucket access work correctly.

4. ERROR PATTERN: All multipart uploads fail at CompleteMultipartUpload with:
   "One or more of the specified parts could not be found. The part may not
   have been uploaded, or the specified entity tag may not match the part's
   entity tag."

   This suggests either:
   a) Parts are not being persisted to storage after upload
   b) Part ETags are being miscalculated/mismatched
   c) There's a bug in how ARMOR tracks uploaded parts internally
